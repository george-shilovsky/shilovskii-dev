5. The level estimate is a simplified model

The estimated level is a one-compartment exponential-decay calculation over the doses you logged and a half-life value. It assumes a dose appears in the body instantly and in full, and it ignores absorption, bioavailability, volume of distribution and route of administration. It is not a blood concentration and must not be used to decide whether, when or how much to take. Half-life values shipped with the app are quoted from published human sources with links; sources may be incomplete, superseded or inapplicable to a particular product or person.
